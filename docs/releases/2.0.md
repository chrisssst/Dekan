# Dekan 2.0

Dekan 2.0 carries forward the existing Dekan application and settings while incorporating current file-format
and injection reliability fixes.

- Read the game's mixed four-byte/eight-byte PROP hash fields without shifting later fields or rewriting wide hashes.
- Refuse injectors that only serve LTK Manager before reporting an active skin, and offer a compatible official release.
- Arm last-second selections immediately and wait for a fresh confirmed hook after reconnecting.
- Re-extract changed or incomplete custom mod archives through a staging folder.
- Bound party sends and share one shutdown deadline across supervised tasks.
- Update Tokio to 1.54.1 and ctutils to 0.4.4, and extend format, injector, extraction, party and reconnect tests.
- Preserve Classic, Crimson and Obsidian themes, Turkish and English, all Dekan logos and Discord presence,
  the default party relay and HTTPS DNS recovery, saved preferences and the update verification key.

The release pipeline selects 2.0 for this explicitly requested major release and stamps the binary, installer
and manifests together. It first publishes a pre-release. Windows build/test results and signed installer
checks are recorded on the release; game-entry and real-match behavior still require a match log.
