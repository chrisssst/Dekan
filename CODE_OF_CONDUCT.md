# Code of Conduct

Dekan follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or identity.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Criticism is fine; insults, harassment and personal attacks are not.
- No sharing of other people's private information, accounts or credentials.
- Do not use this project or its community to cheat against, harass or harm other players.
- Keep discussion of bans, anti-cheat evasion or account trading out of the project spaces.

## Scope

This applies to the GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and to the
project community channel configured by the maintainer.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the contact method configured for your Dekan repository, or
through its private security reporting channel if it involves a
security issue. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and warn, suspend or ban
participants, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
